## Information we collect

### Information you give us

When you fill in a form on this website, such as the free marketing audit, the project enquiry form or the consultation request, we collect the details you enter. Depending on the form, this can include:

- your name, email address and phone or WhatsApp number
- your business name, website and industry
- the services you are interested in and your approximate monthly marketing budget
- any project details or messages you choose to share

We also record which page of the website the form was sent from, so we know what you were looking at when you contacted us.

If you contact us directly by email, phone or WhatsApp, we keep the details you share in that conversation.

### Information collected automatically

**Cookies and similar technologies.** If our analytics and advertising tools are switched on, the website shows a cookie banner. Nothing from these tools loads until you click **Accept**. If you accept, Google Tag Manager may load tools such as Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta Pixel. These use cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as your device and browser type, approximate location, the pages you visit and how you arrived on the site. We use this to understand how the website is used and to measure our own marketing.

If you click **Reject**, these tools are not loaded. You can change your choice at any time using **Cookie settings** in the website footer, or by clearing cookies and site data in your browser.

**Essential storage.** The website stores a small amount of information in your browser that it needs to work, for example your cookie choice and a note that a form was just sent so our thank-you page shows correctly. This information is not used to identify you.

**Technical logs.** Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser and time of request) to keep the site secure and running.

## How we use your information

We use personal information to:

- respond to your enquiry and prepare any audit, proposal or consultation you ask for
- contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about your request
- deliver and manage our services if you become a client
- understand how our website is used and improve it, where you have accepted analytics cookies
- measure the results of our own advertising, where you have accepted marketing cookies
- keep our website secure and meet our legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the details you send through our forms to add you to unrelated marketing lists.

## The basis on which we use it

We process the information you send through our forms on the basis of your consent: you choose to send it to us so that we can respond to your request, and on forms with a consent box you confirm this by ticking it before submitting. We use analytics and advertising cookies only with your consent through the cookie banner. Some limited processing, such as keeping security logs or records we must hold by law, is carried out for legitimate uses permitted under applicable law.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

## Who we share it with

We share personal information only where needed, with:

- **service providers** who help us run the website and our business, such as website hosting, email and form delivery, and, if you accept cookies, analytics and advertising tools (for example Google and Meta). They may only use the information to provide their service to us.
- **professional advisers**, such as accountants or legal advisers, where needed
- **authorities**, where we are required to by law or to protect our rights

Some of these providers may store or process information outside India. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your information stays protected.

If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to respond to you and follow up on your request. If you become a client, we keep the information needed to provide our services and meet our legal and accounting obligations. When we no longer need it, we delete it or make it anonymous.

## How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to this website and limiting access to the people who need it. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to act promptly if something goes wrong.
